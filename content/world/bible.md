# Jokeville — world bible

Every ANEK. story happens in one small town: **Анекдотск** (ru), **Jokeville** (en), **Witzhausen** (de). The audience gets to know its places and residents over many videos, the way viewers know Springfield or South Park. A joke still has to work for someone who has never seen another episode.

## Places (see the town map in context.md)
- **Downtown:** the bar on the corner, the shop (also the pharmacy and the barber), the office tower (bank, police, TV studio), the hospital (doctor's office, ward, psychiatric wing), Main Street with the square, the park, the stadium (and zoo), the cemetery on the edge.
- **Home quarter:** the family flat (living room, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, hallway, balcony, basement), the church, the school (and the university).
- **Outskirts:** the highway, the airport and train station, the harbour.
- **Across the river:** the village with its farm, vegetable garden, forest, lake and mountains.
- **Above and below:** heaven with its gate and St. Peter's desk, the Garden of Eden, outer space; hell underground.

## Residents
Our puppets are townspeople with steady, recognisable traits. Each joke casts them in its roles, but their personality stays the same.
- **Muzhik / Joe / Horst** (`muzhik`): the everyman, simple and sly, fond of a drink; his neighbours look like him (`muzhik-sosed`, chatty and older; `muzhik-ryzhy`, loud).
- **The wife** (`zhena`): sharp-tongued, runs the household, wins every argument.
- **Vovochka / Johnny / Fritzchen** (`vovochka`): the cheeky schoolboy who asks the wrong questions at the right time.
- **The young guy** (`paren`): a student or young professional, a bit naive.
- **The girl** (`devushka`) and her friends (`devushka-blondinka`, `devushka-ryzhaya`): young townswomen.
- **The boss** (`nachalnik`): self-important, pompous.
- **The doctor** (`doktor`): dry, tired, has seen everything.
- **The bartender** (`barmen`): hears every story, ironic.
- **The soldier / policeman** (`sluzhivy`, `sluzhivy-ryadovoy`): by the book.
- **The priest** (`batyushka`, `batyushka-molodoy`), **the teacher** (`uchilka`), **the mother-in-law** (`tyoshcha`), **grandpa** (`ded`), **the little girl** (`devochka`), **the thief** (`vor`), **the visitor from abroad** (`inostranets`).
- **Animals and others:** the dog, the cat, the parrot, the cow, the horse, the devil.

## Rules for writers
- Use the town naturally: "at the bar on the corner" or "the town doctor" is fine. Don't explain the town and don't force callbacks.
- Never add ethnic, national or religious stereotypes the joke does not have (rule 11). Never ridicule faith itself. A priest can be a flawed person; God, scripture and sacraments are never the punchline.
- One joke, one punchline, at the end. Scenes and props serve the joke; they are not decoration.
