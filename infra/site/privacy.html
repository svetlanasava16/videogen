Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20

ANEK. is a publishing tool used only by its operators to post their own videos and texts to their own social media accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Threads and others).

What we access

When an operator connects an account, the platform gives us an access token and basic profile information (account id, name, avatar). We use them only to publish content the operator has prepared and to read statistics (views, likes, comments, shares) of those posts.

What we store

Access tokens, the list of scheduled and published posts, and post statistics, on servers controlled by the operators. We do not collect data about viewers, do not sell or share data, and do not use it for advertising.

Deletion

Disconnecting an account in the tool or revoking access in the platform's settings removes the token. To request deletion of all stored data, contact the operators via the contact listed on the connected channel.
